Financial Literacy

Financial Terms Explained Simply

Demystifying common economic and market terminology in plain English.

REPO RATE

Repo Rate

The benchmark interest rate at which the central bank (like RBI or Fed) lends money to commercial banks.

INFLATION

Inflation

The rate at which general prices for goods and services rise over time, reducing purchasing power.

MARKET CAP

Market Capitalization

The total dollar value of a company's outstanding shares of stock in the market.

EBITDA

EBITDA

Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization — a measure of operational profit.

BULL MARKET

Bull Market

A financial market condition where stock prices are continuously rising or expected to rise.

CPI

Consumer Price Index

A statistical metric examining price changes in a basket of everyday consumer goods and services.

VOLATILITY

Market Volatility

The speed and magnitude of price changes for a stock or market index over a given period.

DIVIDEND

Dividend

A portion of corporate profits paid directly to shareholders as a return on investment.
